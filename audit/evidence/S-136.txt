TasteProbe
Tasting room

METHOD

How a flight is poured
01
The flight, course by course

Seven steps from a venue name to a measured glass. Each one is a real call or a computation you can replay from the cached responses.

01
Source the palates
QLOO TASTE AI

Qloo /search resolves the venue in its city, then /v2/insights returns taste tags with affinity for that city, topped up from the venue’s global neighbourhood when the city has fewer than six (each audit says how many). If the key refuses urn:tag, as the hackathon host may, the palates come from artist, brand, film, book, TV, podcast and place affinities instead.

02
Seat the personas
≥ 8 PER GLASS

Each persona anchors on one taste signal and blends in two affinity-weighted ones. Signals are grouped into taste segments: by Qloo tag subtype, or, on the hackathon key (urn:entity only), by entity type, such as music, film, brand or place taste. Each segment gets at least eight personas.

03
Pour the question
BRAND NEVER NAMED

The baseline prompt holds only the persona’s tastes, the category and the city. It never names you, so every mention is earned.

04
Take tasting notes
NVIDIA NEMOTRON

Every assistant answer is a tasting note. Nemotron on Nebius extracts the venues it recommends, cross-checked against a plain numbered-list parser.

05
Measure the glass
WILSON 95%

Each glass fills to your share of the answers. The pour lines are a Wilson interval on a persona-adjusted sample size, because one persona’s answers move together.

06
Re-pour three flights
PAIRED BOOTSTRAP

Nemotron writes copy for the weakest glass. The same personas re-ask every assistant three ways at once: baseline, a placebo that only names you, and the fix.

07
Keep the sommelier’s notebook
TOOL CALLING

A Nemotron agent revises copy with four tools under a budget. Every attempt is written down. Its intervals only screen; a win needs one confirmation probe (on held-out personas from 16 per segment, otherwise a fresh re-ask of the same personas), and the stop rule runs in code, not in the model.

02
Reading a glass

Every number on the board has one meaning. Nothing is smoothed, ranked or scored.

EXAMPLE, NOT A REAL AUDIT
fill · 31%
upper pour line · 47%
lower pour line · 18%
Fill level
The share of usable answers in this taste segment that recommend you. The scale is linear: the 50 tick is half the answers.
Pour lines
The 95% Wilson interval, widened by a design effect for persona clustering. Zero mentions still leaves a gap above the base, never a 0–0% line.
Placard
The segment’s Qloo tag subtype, its share and its interval. Calls that failed or could not be parsed are counted there and kept out of the denominator.
Tasting notes
The raw assistant answers behind the glass, each with the venues Nemotron extracted and a flag where the list parser disagrees.
03
Re-pour, three ways

A fix is tested as a prompt-level counterfactual: the copy is injected as retrieved context. It is not a published website change.

EXAMPLE, NOT A REAL AUDIT

03
Fix

Lift = fix − placebo, with a 95% paired bootstrap interval over personas. The placebo names you without the copy, so the lift measures the copy rather than the name.

SOMMELIER’S NOTEBOOK
RULES, NOT A RUN
Tools live Qloo insights · propose copy · probe the segment · read the stats
Probe placebo against fix on the segment’s next personas
Stop after one confirmation probe once a copy clears the screen: on held-out personas the search never used, or, in segments under 16 personas, a fresh re-ask of the same personas; or when tries, calls, tokens or dollars run out
Enforced in code, not by the model; every attempt stays in the notebook
